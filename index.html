FAQ & policies

Good to know

What areas do you serve?
We're based in Easley and regularly serve Easley, Pendleton, Clemson and nearby Upstate towns. Addresses more than 20 miles from Easley have a $20 travel fee. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.
When do you set up and take down?
We set up the night before your event date and take the display down the following evening, so it's up for about 24 hours. Depending on our removal schedule it may stay a little longer. Want more days? Add them for $35 each.
How do I pay and reserve my date?
After you send a request, we'll contact you to go over details and availability. Payment by Venmo or Zelle at the time of booking reserves your date and signs.
What if it rains?
Safety first: we can't set up in lightning, thunderstorms, heavy rain or winds over 10 mph. If weather keeps us from setting up, you'll get a full refund.
What's your cancellation policy?
If you need to cancel, you can take a 50% refund or keep the full amount as credit for a future date. Refunds aren't available if the address is wrong, the homeowner asks us to remove the display, we're denied access to a gated community, animals prevent setup, or other circumstances outside our control stop the install.
Rental terms (please read)
  1. Payment is required at booking to reserve your date and signs.
  2. Missing or damaged display pieces will be invoiced.
  3. Please don't let anyone play on, lean on or sit on the display. It won't support a child's or adult's weight.
  4. Please don't move the pieces or pull them from the ground. Photos next to them are encouraged!
  5. Please don't throw away or damage any pieces. If the honoree isn't a fan, let us know and we'll remove it.
  6. Protect the display from lawnmowers, string trimmers and pets. If a lawn crew is coming, have them come before our setup.
  7. Please don't attach balloons. They heat up in the sun and can damage our signs when they pop.
  8. Lawnfetti isn't liable for injury or damage during transport, setup, removal or return of a rental.
Can you do something that's not on your list?
Absolutely. We love to celebrate anything and everything. Tell us what you have in mind in the booking form and we'll make it happen if we can.
